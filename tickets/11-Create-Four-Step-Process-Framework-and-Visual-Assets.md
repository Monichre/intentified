## Technical Specifications

### Four-Step Process Framework

```
## How We Turn Intent Into Revenue

### 1. LISTEN - Trillions of Signals

We monitor **50 billion URLs daily** across **270 million US consumers**, tracking real-time buying behavior from:

- Competitor websites
- Social media engagement  
- Search queries
- Review sites
- Industry publications

### 2. DISCOVER - Extreme Precision

Our AI identifies prospects actively researching solutions like yours:

- Which competitors they're visiting
- What products they're comparing
- When they're ready to buy
- Full contact details (name, email, phone)

### 3. CREATE - Automated Excellence

Personalized campaigns created in real-time:

- Dynamic content based on intent signals
- Industry-specific messaging
- Behavior-triggered sequences
- Multi-channel coordination

### 4. SEND - Speed to Lead

Reach prospects while they're still shopping:

- Near real-time email delivery (5 minutes or less)
- 8 different ISPs for maximum deliverability
- Automated text follow-up for hot leads
- Direct appointment scheduling
```

### Speed-to-Lead Timeline Visualization

```
T+0 min: Prospect visits competitor site
T+2 min: Identity resolved and qualified
T+3 min: Personalized content created
T+5 min: Email delivered to inbox
T+15 min: Follow-up sequence activated
T+1 hour: SMS outreach (if opted in)
T+24 hrs: Multi-touch nurture begins
```
