## Drip Sequence Configuration

### Timing
- **Email 1:** Immediate (Day 0)
- **Email 2:** Day 2
- **Email 3:** Day 5  
- **Email 4:** Day 8
- **Email 5:** Day 12

### Segmentation
- **New Signups:** Full sequence
- **Demo Requests:** Skip to Email 4-5
- **Trial Users:** Separate onboarding sequence
- **Existing Customers:** Exclude from sequence

### Personalization Variables
- `[First Name]` - Subscriber's first name
- `[Company]` - Company name (if available)
- `[Industry]` - Industry vertical
- `[Current Platform]` - Current email platform (if known)

### Call-to-Action Buttons
- **[Book Your Demo]** - Links to calendar booking
- **[View HTML Templates]** - Links to template gallery
- **[Start Your Free Trial]** - Links to trial signup
- **[Book a 15-minute call]** - Links to consultation booking

### Success Metrics to Track
- **Open Rates:** Target 42.35% (industry median)
- **Click-Through Rates:** Target 4.67% (automated flow average)
- **Conversion Rates:** Track demo bookings and trial signups
- **Unsubscribe Rate:** Keep below 2%

### A/B Testing Opportunities
- Subject line variations
- Send time optimization
- CTA button text and placement
- Email length (short vs. detailed)
- Social proof elements

### Follow-up Actions
- **Demo Booked:** Move to sales sequence
- **Trial Started:** Move to onboarding sequence  
- **No Engagement:** Move to re-engagement campaign
- **Unsubscribed:** Remove from all sequences
